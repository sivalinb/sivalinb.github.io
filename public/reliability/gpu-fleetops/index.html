A measurement contract you can inspect.

What does MTBI mean here?

Operating entity-hours divided by observed interruption episodes. GPU, node and job schedules are explicit and separate. Duplicate records and overlapping outages do not multiply interruptions. Maintenance and unknown telemetry are excluded from operating exposure.

How are healthy devices and missing telemetry handled?

Kaplan–Meier includes devices still healthy at the end. The first telemetry gap right-censors the first-event observation. Weibull uses a censored likelihood and requires enough failures; its pooled fit remains descriptive.
